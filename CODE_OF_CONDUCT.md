# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must keep discussion technical, respectful, and
free of harassment. Do not publish personal data, credentials, private session
content, or material that a contributor has asked to keep confidential.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained
disruption of project discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to every project space and to public representations of
the project. Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or escalate contributions
that violate it.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact
address published on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not use public issues for
reports that identify a person or contain private material. Security reports
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
